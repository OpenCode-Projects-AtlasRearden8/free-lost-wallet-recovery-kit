# Fraud Note Template — Disputes and Notes to Banks

Two short, plain letters: one for card issuers (unauthorized charges), one for anything else (insurance, employer, or a bank that needs context). Keep them short — one screen, no emotion, just facts. Attach the police report number if you have one.

**This is general information, not legal or financial advice.** Substitute the bracketed details with your own. Do not fill in a real version of this and share it publicly.

---

## Letter 1: Card issuer — unauthorized charge dispute

**To:** The address (or secure-message window) shown on your statement for "disputes" or "fraud."

> **Subject: Unauthorized charge dispute — card ending [XXXX]**
>
> Dear [Bank / Issuer name],
>
> I am writing to dispute a charge on the account listed above.
>
> - Charge amount: $[XX.XX]
> - Merchant name as it appears on my statement: [MERCHANT]
> - Date of the charge: [MM/DD/YYYY]
>
> I did not make, authorize, or benefit from this charge. Card ending **[XXXX]** was lost on or about [DATE] and I reported it lost/stolen on [DATE]. Your reference number for that report is [CONFIRMATION #]. [Optional: "A police report was filed on [DATE]; report number [REPORT #]."]
>
> I am asking that:
> 1. The charge be removed from the account per your fraud and zero-liability policies, and
> 2. A written confirmation of the dispute outcome be sent to me.
>
> Sincerely,
> [Your name]
> [Mailing address on file] — safer: send through the bank's secure message window
> Daytime phone: [phone]

**Send it from inside the bank's app or website** ("secure message" / "dispute a charge") whenever possible — it timestamps the message and keeps everything in one place. Mail is the backup if the app has no dispute path.

## Letter 2: Context note — other organizations

Use this when you need to explain the situation to someone who isn't a bank: an employer (badge), an insurer, a subscription that charged a dead card, or your state DMV.

> **Subject: Wallet lost on [DATE] — account [ACCOUNT/LAST-4]**
>
> Hello,
>
> My wallet was lost or possibly stolen on or about [DATE]. It contained [ITEM], which affects this account/record.
>
> What I have already done:
> 1. Reported the cards missing to my bank(s) on [DATE].
> 2. Placed fraud alerts / credit freezes with the three credit bureaus on [DATE].
> 3. [Filed a police report on [DATE], report number [___], if applicable.]
>
> What I need from you:
> - [Replace the card / badge now attached to this account.]
> - [Confirm when the replacement will arrive.]
> - [Flag the account in case of unusual activity.]
>
> Thank you,
> [Your name]
> [Preferred contact method]

## Tips that keep disputes moving

1. **Dispute within 2 business days** when you can — card-network rules give the strongest protection the sooner you report.
2. **One-sentence story.** "Card was lost on [date]; I reported it on [date]; I did not make this charge." Anything longer slows the desk.
3. **Ask for the dispute confirmation number every time**, and log it in `call-list.md`.
4. **Keep it in writing** where possible. A secure message beats a phone call; a phone call then a follow-up message is even better.
5. **Follow up at 10 business days** if you've heard nothing. That's a normal time to check in, not an escalation.
