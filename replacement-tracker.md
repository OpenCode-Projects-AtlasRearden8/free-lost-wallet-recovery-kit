# Replacement Order Tracker — Cards and IDs

One row per item. Fill it in as you order replacements, and don't cross it off until it's in your hand. This page is your sanity when the pile of envelopes arrives out of order.

**Rule of thumb:** order debit cards and your driver's license first — everything else re-does itself in priority order after that.

- [ ] Phone wallet (Apple Pay / Google Pay / Samsung Pay) re-add the new card
- [ ] Recurring-securities / parking / gym — call to attach the new card
- [ ] Bank app: confirm the new card's limit and PIN work
- [ ] Shred or confidentially destroy the old (dead) cards if any show up in the wash a week later
