# Call List — Lost Wallet Edition

Fill this in **before** you need it if you're reading this calmly... or fill it in right now using bank websites and apps if you're mid-emergency. Phone numbers should always be verified on the company's official website or app — never from a letter, text, or email you didn't expect.

**Tip:** Most call scripts for lost cards are the same. Say: *"My wallet is lost. I need to freeze or cancel my card ending in ___, and get a replacement sent."* They'll walk you through the rest.

Recency rule: debit cards and anything directly tied to your bank account — call these first.

## What to ask on every card call

1. Freeze or cancel-and-replace? (Ask for: what keeps your account, and blocks nobody but you.)
2. Has there been any activity since the card went missing?
3. Am I liable for charges? (Written confirmation, if possible, even by email/chat.)
4. How fast does the replacement card arrive, and can it be expedited?
5. Confirmation number: ______________________________________________
