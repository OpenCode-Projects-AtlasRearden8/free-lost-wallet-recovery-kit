# Prevention Sheet — The "Never Again" 30 Minutes

The emergency is handled. Do this sheet once, tonight or this weekend, and the next lost wallet becomes a 30-minute phone-call problem instead of a 30-day dread problem. Nothing here costs money and nothing leaves your house.

---

## 1. Slim the wallet down (10 minutes)

Dump your wallet on the table. Put back only what you used in the last 30 days.

**Goes in the wallet:**

- [ ] One debit card
- [ ] One credit card (a different issuer if you have a choice — if one bank has an outage, you still have a card)
- [ ] Driver's license / state ID
- [ ] Your transit card if you actually use it
- [ ] Maybe: one membership card you truly use

**Moves to a locked drawer at home:**

- [ ] Spare credit cards (they still work even if they're in your drawer)
- [ ] Social Security card — it never needed to be in there
- [ ] Chequebook, if you still carry one
- [ ] Medicare/insurance cards you can reprint from an app when needed
- [ ] Old gift cards, loyalty cards, receipts, business cards
- [ ] Anything with your full name + address + a photo together (that combo is the identity-theft jackpot)

## 2. Make the "vault" (10 minutes)

One paper document, one photo, one backup — that's the whole vault.

- [ ] **Write a "wallet inventory" card** (or use the back of this sheet): every card's issuer + last 4 digits + the bank's phone number from the back of the card. Store at home — **not** in the wallet.
- [ ] **Photograph your wallet contents** with your phone: front and back of each card. **Cover or crop the CVV (3-digit security code) and photos of faces.** Store the photos in the private photo album on your own device only — not in a shared cloud album.
- [ ] **One emergency card lives at home** (or with a trusted person) — the drawer card from step 1 counts.
- [ ] **House/car keys:** decide the rule — keys never live in the wallet, ever. A key ring that attaches to your belt or bag loop is the cheap fix.

## 3. Phone wallet + alerts (5 minutes)

- [ ] **Add one card to your phone wallet** (Apple Pay / Google Pay / Samsung Pay). Losing a wallet with your phone in your pocket still leaves you with a way to pay.
- [ ] **Turn on push alerts** for every card transaction: bank app → notifications → "all transactions." This is the alert that would have caught it in aisle 4 instead of day 3.
- [ ] **Lock screen: set "Find My" / location for your own phone** so a lost phone doesn't cascade.

## 4. Recovery plan on paper (5 minutes)

Print or hand-copy the first page of `checklist.md` and `call-list.md` — the "first 30 minutes" list and the bank-phone-number blanks. Tuck this in with your vault.

The whole point: **the next emergency starts with reading your own instructions at home instead of reconstructing phone numbers from memory in a parking lot.**

## Quick re-visit: every 6 months

- [ ] Swap the drawer card if you've been using your wallet card and vice versa (keeps the drawer card alive)
- [ ] Re-slim the wallet (it grows back; this is normal)
- [ ] Refresh the wallet-inventory card if anything changed banks or numbers
