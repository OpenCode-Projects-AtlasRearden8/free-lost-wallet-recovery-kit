# Police Report Template — Lost or Stolen Wallet

Use this when the wallet was stolen, your driver's license is gone, or a bank asks for a report number. Many jurisdictions take these reports online; some ask you to come in. Either way, the station mostly needs a short, factual summary — this template is that summary. Bring it filled out and you'll be in and out.

**This page is a template with invented placeholders — do not put real personal data into a version you share or commit.**

---

## The one-paragraph version (what you say at the desk or online)

> "I am reporting a lost wallet, and I believe it may have been stolen. It went missing on or about [DATE] at or near [LOCATION]. It contained: [LIST OF ITEMS BELOW]. I have already reported the missing cards to my bank and card issuers on [DATE]. I am requesting a report number for my records and for my bank's fraud claim. My contact details are listed below."

**Do NOT bring or read out** your Social Security Number, account numbers, or passwords. The report does not need them, and less on paper is safer. If a form requires a license number and you don't have it, say so — they can process it without.

## After you leave

- [ ] Write the report number into `call-list.md` and any fraud claims (banks ask for it)
- [ ] Email yourself the photo of the report, if one was printed
- [ ] If your license was in the wallet, keep a photocopy of the report with your FTC identity-theft paperwork if you file one at IdentityTheft.gov
