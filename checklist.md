# What-to-Do Checklist: Lost or Stolen Wallet

A step-by-step list for the day your wallet goes missing. Work top to bottom — the order matters. Check boxes as you go. It's okay to skip a step and come back.

**Rule #1: Freeze first, think second.** Freezing costs nothing and un-freezes in minutes. Nobody will be annoyed that you acted too fast.

First, take a breath. 30 seconds. Then start.

---

## The first 30 minutes

- [ ] **Retrace, briefly.** Give pockets, car, last three places you visited, and the couch a 5–10 minute look. Do not spend more than this right now.
- [ ] **Check if it was stolen, not lost.** Missing cash + still have your phone? Probably lost. Phone also gone, or cards already have charges? That's theft — see the police-report template.
- [ ] **Look for early charges.** Log into your two most-used card apps and check the last few transactions. New charge you didn't make = act faster below.
- [ ] **Decide your story.** "Lost" vs. "stolen" changes what you say to banks and police. Pick one word and stick with it. If unsure, say "lost or possibly stolen."

## Freeze everything: the first hour

- [ ] **Call each debit card's bank to freeze or cancel.** Debit cards are riskier than credit — act on these first. Use numbers from your bank's app or website, not the drawer where your wallet used to be. (Fill in `call-list.md` as you go.)
- [ ] **Call each credit card issuer to freeze or cancel.** Ask: freeze or cancel-and-replace? Freeze keeps the account; cancel-and-replace changes the card number.
- [ ] **Freeze your credit reports (free, takes ~10 min each):**
  - [ ] Equifax freeze
  - [ ] Experian fraud alert (one bureau and it notifies the others — start here if short on time)
  - [ ] TransUnion freeze
- [ ] **Lock your phone wallet.** If you used Apple Pay / Google Pay / Samsung Pay, those tokenized card numbers are often still usable for some transit/purchases — call your bank to disable them (they usually do this automatically when you report the card).
- [ ] **Change your banking app PIN/passwords** if your wallet contained a paper list of passwords (it shouldn't anymore — see the prevention sheet).

## The rest of the first day

- [ ] **Check every account that had a card saved.** Streaming, rideshare, online stores. Update the saved card after replacements arrive; remove the dead card now if you can.
- [ ] **Subscription autopay.** Make a list of anything that auto-charges a vanished card. The replacement tracker has rows for this.
- [ ] **File a police report if:**
  - You believe it was stolen,
  - A driver's license + other IDs are gone (helps with ID-theft protection), or
  - Any bank requires a report number for fraud claims. Use `police-report-template.md`.
- [ ] **Tell your employer** if work badges, HSA cards, or parking cards were in the wallet.
- [ ] **Back up the story.** Email yourself a short note: date, time noticed, what was in the wallet, who you called. You'll want this for disputes later.

## Days 2–7

- [ ] **Order replacements.** Work the `replacement-tracker.md`, one row at a time:
  - [ ] Debit card(s)
  - [ ] Credit card(s)
  - [ ] Driver's license / state ID (most states allow online replacement ordering)
  - [ ] Health insurance card
  - [ ] Auto insurance card (many insurers offer a digital version you can use right away)
  - [ ] Library card, gym card, work badge, transit card
  - [ ] Social Security card — **only if needed for a job or benefits**; it lives better at home (prevention sheet)
- [ ] **Watch accounts daily.** Fraud can appear weeks later. A 2-minute scan of each account, once a day, this week.
- [ ] **Set alerts.** Turn on text/email alerts for every transaction over $0 on replaced cards.
- [ ] **If your driver's license was stolen:** consider an FTC identity-theft report at IdentityTheft.gov — it creates a recovery plan and printable affidavit for free.

## Week 2 and after

- [ ] **Follow up on any disputes** with `fraud-note-template.md` if needed.
- [ ] **Confirm all replacements arrived** (tracker should have zero open rows).
- [ ] **Do the prevention sheet.** `prevention-sheet.md` — 30 minutes, one time, and a lost wallet becomes a 30-minute problem instead of a 30-day problem.

---

## The panic script (read this out loud if frozen)

> "My wallet is missing. I am calling my banks, in order of damage risk. I freeze first and figure out the rest later. Freezing is free. Reversing fraud after 2 days is harder. Phone numbers come from bank websites and apps, not from memory."
